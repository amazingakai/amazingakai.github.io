# Hey, I'm Azhar 👋

I'm a computer science student interested in low level programming,
distributed systems, and observability tooling. I write mostly C++,
Rust, Go, and Python.

## What I'm up to right now

I'm an **LFX Mentee** at OpenTelemetry, working on
[Go Compile Time Instrumentation](https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-go-compile-instrumentation)
under the guidance of [Kemal Akkoyun](https://github.com/kakkoyun) and
[Dario Castañé](https://github.com/darccio). I stumbled into observability
while trying to add tracing to a Discord minigame I was building. I had
no idea what I was doing, and I ended up here.

I also daily-drive **KDE Plasma** and contribute to KDE apps and
libraries like KArchive and NeoChat. I was a **GSoC 2025 mentee** at
KDE, working on OSS-Fuzz under the guidance of
[Albert Astals Cid](https://invent.kde.org/aacid).

I write blog posts about the things I'm working on and learning along
the way, check them out [here](/posts).

When I'm not at a keyboard, I'm probably losing at chess.
